# Solutions

Worked solutions appear in this folder **one week behind**.

When you sync on Wednesday, you get:

- this week's todo file and tests, **and**
- **last week's finished `game.py`**, as `solutions/weekNN_game.py`

Last week's submission closes Tuesday at midnight, so by the time a solution
lands here, that week is already graded. Nothing you can still earn points for
is ever sitting in this folder.

## What it's for

Catching up — not checking your answer before you try.

If last week beat you, or you were out, open the solution, take what you need
into your `game.py`, and keep moving. **Falling a week behind is recoverable.
Falling three weeks behind is not.** That's the whole reason this folder exists.

You'll score 0 for a week you skipped, but full marks on everything after it.

## How to use one

Open `solutions/weekNN_game.py` beside your own `game.py` and compare. Copy
across what you're missing.

Don't replace your whole file unless you have nothing — you'd lose your own
colors, numbers and story, and the solution is deliberately plain so that
yours can be better than it.
